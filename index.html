About The Nook

The Story Nook is a soft little writing ritual: watch the weekly inspiration, draft a flash-fiction response, then read, vote, and comment on what others make.

  • Watch: ease into the mood with the weekly video.
  • Write: keep a local draft, use prompt seeds, and publish when ready.
  • Connect: vote, comment, collect badges, and keep your favorite pen name.
